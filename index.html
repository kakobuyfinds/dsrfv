02 / Make the sheet useful

How to use an OopBuy spreadsheet

An OopBuy spreadsheet is a product research table: names, categories, listing references and comparison notes arranged in rows. Its value comes from recording the exact option you checked. A picture and a price are a starting point; size, availability and the complete cost decide whether an item belongs on your shortlist.

This page uses CAFANS as its catalog source. The shopping buttons take you there to continue browsing. They do not place an OopBuy order, and inclusion here does not establish that a particular listing can currently be purchased through OopBuy. Confirm agent availability and the original listing before paying.

Choose a category

Start with shoes, tops or jackets. Filter the selection to compare similar items rather than unrelated prices.

Identify the exact item

Keep the CAFANS item number, source link, color and chosen size together so you can return to the same listing.

Resolve missing details

Request measurements, confirm the current price and check whether the seller supports the service you intend to use.

Review the final cost

Add confirmed delivery and service charges. Review any available QC photos and applicable return terms before shipment.

05 / Clear answers

OopBuy spreadsheet FAQ

Is this the official OopBuy website?

No. This is an independent discovery guide with a selected CAFANS catalog snapshot. OopBuy and CAFANS are referenced by name to explain the research topic and the product source. The page does not claim an official partnership.

Where do the product names, photographs and prices come from?

The six entries were selected from the CAFANS public homepage. Their item numbers identify the source listings. Prices are the source’s estimated USD amounts at the snapshot date; this page does not continuously synchronize inventory or prices.

Where do “OopBuy spreadsheet” and “Buy it now” take me?

Both buttons take you to https://cafans.com. Browse there and locate your chosen item using its name or item number. Confirm the current product details and supported buying options before continuing.

Can I download a spreadsheet?

Yes. With JavaScript enabled, use “Export visible rows” above the product grid. The CSV includes the currently filtered selection, indicative USD prices, original CAFANS product URLs and empty fields for your own size, color and comparison notes.

Are the displayed prices the full amount I will pay?

No. They are indicative item prices. Delivery, service fees, currency conversion and other applicable charges may change the total. Use the planner with your own quotes and leave unconfirmed charges marked as unknown.

Does a product listing include QC or a fit guarantee?

No. This directory has not verified individual QC records. Ask for relevant evidence and compare measurements for your selected option. A seller photograph, warehouse photograph and independent inspection provide different kinds of information.

What should I do if a saved listing changes?

Check that the product number, photographs and selected variant still match your notes. Update the price and checked date. If the listing is unavailable or the details cannot be confirmed, mark that row as unresolved instead of treating it as ready to order.
